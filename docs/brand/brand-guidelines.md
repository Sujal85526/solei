Direction: burgundy dominant, pink used thoughtfully; unisex, sophisticated, not overly feminine, not overwhelming.

## Typography
- Brand font: **"Nove" (Canva)**. Need the font files + a **web licence** — Canva fonts can't be assumed usable on a website.
  Unverified which typeface this is (a MyFonts "Nove" by Fabrizio Schiavi/FSD exists, but it's geometric, unlike the profile's serifs).
  Ask client for a screenshot of text set in Nove to choose a close web alternative if needed.
- Company profile uses a high-contrast serif for headings, serif italics for taglines, letter-spaced caps for labels.

## Logo
- Custom lowercase "solei" wordmark; pink on burgundy (ref-3) and burgundy on cream (profile). Need SVG/vector files.

## Story, vision, tagline
- Tagline: **Made to remain.**
- Story: born from a love of fragrance + travel; every scent is tied to a destination (notes, name, packaging).
  "Fragrance is more than something you wear. It's a memory, a feeling, and a little piece of somewhere that stays with you."
- Vision: fragrance house beyond the traditional — fragrance + travel + storytelling; every purchase feels like an experience.
- Profile lines: "A destination in scent." · "Every bottle is an invitation to remember a place differently." ·
  "Collect the destinations. Keep the memory."
- Based in Abu Dhabi, UAE · Instagram @solei.ad

## Collection (from profile)
- 8 destination scents: **Cannes, Marbella, Verona, Capri, Soho, Monaco, Mayfair, Santorini**
- 5 perfumes (glass bottles, burgundy ball caps) — **AED 280**
- 3 all-over sprays (pink tubes, gold band, 100 ml; e.g. Soho) — **AED 190**
- Which scents are perfume vs spray: to confirm.

## Solei Passport (existing physical loyalty programme)
- 1 sticker per product purchased (destination stickers)
- Passport given after 2 purchases or on request
- **12 stickers = 1 free all-over spray of choice**
- Decision: launch with these rules, all configurable from admin (see requirements.md).

## Website design direction
- Luxurious, minimal, visually memorable; "luxury travel editorial"
- Subtle travel motifs (profile uses: arches with palm, postmarks/postage stamps, airplane stamp, postcard-style
  destination cards, passport) — subtle, not themed/crowded
- Beautiful product photography, elegant type, unique to Solei (not a typical perfume site)
- Destination concept woven into shopping (shop by destination)
